      How Situation Guard works

      Alexa proposes. A strict engine decides. You approve.

      • Your plan is a set of commitments: who does what, when and where, with which shared things (a TV, a room), and what matters most.
      • The engine checks rules: one person can’t be in two places, a shared TV fits one show, a deadline is a deadline, nothing happens in the past, a closed or rained-out place can’t be used.
      • Reality comes in from your location, your Google Calendar, live weather, public holidays and live transport.
      • Fixes are tested before they’re offered. Alexa only says an option works if the engine said so, and changes your plan (and calendar) only after your yes.

      Every action is an MCP tool call to the Situation Guard server, exactly as Alexa+ or any MCP client would make it.
